Privacy policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

AikaTrail is an offline time tracker for iPhone and iPad, made by an individual developer. The app has no account, advertising, or analytics, and it sends no data to the developer. For privacy questions or requests, email konsta.support@gmail.com.

Data in the app

AikaTrail stores your activities, tags, sessions, and settings on your device. iCloud or computer backups may include this data. If you export or share data, the destination you choose handles its copy under its own policy.

AikaTrail keeps a small error log with error details, timestamps, and the app version and build. The log contains no activity names or session history. The app marks the diagnostics folder for exclusion from system backups and removes entries older than seven days when it maintains the log.

You can delete activities, tags, and sessions in the app. Deleting the app removes its local data. Backups and exports can retain separate copies that you can manage in your backup settings or wherever you saved them.
